# Tags

One tag per line, `- tag: meaning`. `scripts/kb.py check` rejects tags missing here.

- pqc: post-quantum cryptography and migration
- tls: TLS, certificates, handshakes, network protocols
- regulation: laws, standards, deadlines, government mandates
- market: competitors, funding, market size, buyers
- customer: who pays, interviews, buyer behaviour
- agents: coding agents, their capabilities and failure rates
- quantum: quantum computing hardware and timelines
- hackathon: Garaža rules, judging, logistics
- idea: candidate directions, kept or dropped
- demo: what we show and how
- human: needs a human answer
