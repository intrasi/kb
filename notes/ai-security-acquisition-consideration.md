`EV-PROTECTAI-CONSIDERATION-2025` is Palo Alto Networks’ disclosed total purchase consideration for Protect AI. `EV-LAKERA-CONSIDERATION-2026` is the official Lakera purchase consideration in preliminary acquisition accounting, excluding separately treated post-combination compensation. `EV-LAKERA-MEDIA-ESTIMATE-2025` is a media estimate. The article says the price was not disclosed. It is not the official accounting amount, and this file keeps both records.
