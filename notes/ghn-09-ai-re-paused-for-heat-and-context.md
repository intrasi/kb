asimovDev says an AI-assisted reversing project was put on hold because the machine ran at full fan speed for hours, and that the run had used about 200k tokens of context three times. That contradicts a claim that current AI reversing is frictionless. It does not contradict the existence of the threat, and it does not show that the attempt failed.

The same archived comment, not the excerpt above, says the author has "very little experience doing grown up reverse engineering". That is a visible self-description, not a seniority judgment.
