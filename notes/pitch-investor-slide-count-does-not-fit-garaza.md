The checklist recommends about 15 to 20 slides, including 2 to 3 on tactics and 3 on
financials and contingencies, for a venture raise. Garaža gives 3 minutes, and up to 1
of them is the demo ([[garaza-pitch-three-minutes-one-minute-demo]]). At about 20
seconds per slide, that leaves room for roughly 6 slides.

Keep from the checklist: narrative first, headlines as claims, one message per slide, no
clutter, proof instead of superlatives, honesty about unknowns. Move go-to-market detail,
financials, and future hires into Q&A backup slides. The judging criteria
([[garaza-judging-six-criteria]]) reward demand, originality, and ambition, so those
earn the main slides.
