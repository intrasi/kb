- Open format, but the problem "must be a real problem with global relevance, not a
  local one".
- A working MVP that presents well beats a polished product.
- Solutions only for regional markets such as the Balkans are discouraged.
- Industries to avoid: PetTech, KidTech and FamilyTech, EdTech (workplace education is
  fine), and GovTech. Building there is not disqualifying but "will impact the judging
  criteria".
- The organisers point teams to their Request for Startups list, their What Not to Build
  list, the Tarpit MCP (also in this repo as the /tarpit skill), and the Garaža blog.

Why it matters to us: code protection is global and outside every avoided industry. A
pitch framed around government or public-sector buyers would drift toward GovTech, so
lead with fintech, games, and mobile.
