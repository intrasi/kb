PACE's Fusion documentation describes profile-based exclusion of frequently called functions, developer exclusions, training builds and automated tuning. Its July 8, 2026 Fusion 6 announcement emphasizes easier integration and standalone protection without iLok licensing. This supplies a concrete baseline for a proposed optimizer: incumbents already use workload information and automate build protection. These are product descriptions, not independently measured engineering savings.

PACE's September 10, 2025 Fusion AI announcement targets deployed model protection in audio, broadcasting and post-production. Here **AI refers to the protected asset**, not evidence that an AI agent invents obfuscation. The release establishes an offering, not a named model-protection buyer or measured demand. Do not reuse its economy-wide IP-theft figure as our market size.

Thales' Java Envelope guide still requires application-specific method selection and documents performance and lifecycle hazards. This is evidence of a configuration surface, scoped to Java Envelope; it does not prove every modern native protector has this limitation.

Opportunity hypothesis: reduce repeat tuning and regression investigation while independently measuring attack outcomes. A generic agent choosing settings is insufficient differentiation without measured improvement.
