The checklist asks founders to be "brutally honest about what you know and don't know"
and to frame risk as "If I can prove X, then [massive outcome Y] happens".

Applied to code protection, the open premises in
[[code-protection-field-open-questions]] become the frame, for example: "If attacker
cost measured against current AI models holds up across apps, then every company that
ships client-side code needs that number before it ships." This also prepares the 5
minutes of judge questions.
