The sourced market records are Karla's: [[in-app-protection-and-shielding-estimates]] and
[[application-security-market-estimates]], indexed in [[code-protection-evidence-index]]. Use those
for any figure. This note adds three things.

1. **$16.2B has no source.** An earlier team pitch used it as an in-app protection endpoint. No
   publisher was found for it. The nearest figures are for all of application security (about
   $16.5B). Do not use it.
2. **CMI publishes two series that don't agree:**
   - $1.8B (2024) → $7.2B (2034) at 18%, from an older page version found through search engines;
   - $2.1B (2025) → $2.7B (2026) → $8.9B (2035) at 17.7%, from its press release.

   The 28% jump from 2025 to 2026 is unexplained. Cite the year and version with any CMI figure.
3. **MarketsandMarkets may have revised its application-security figure.** A March 2026 release
   said $41.16B for 2026; search snippets suggest a September 2026 revision to $13.63B, which we
   could not open. This matches Karla's "undated, cutoff-unverified" label. Tracked in
   [[re-research-gaps-to-verify]].
