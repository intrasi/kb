Apple's Foundation Models framework offers on-device "AI inference that is free of cost", and the
featured apps name no paid tiers. One developer blog describes on-device generation as "completely
free" with the paid tier using cloud AI. Search snippets show the same pattern elsewhere; one app
(Privacy AI) charges for larger local models (snippet).

Low confidence: a few examples, not a survey.

Why it matters: "premium AI features run on the phone, so protect the app" is not yet supported.
