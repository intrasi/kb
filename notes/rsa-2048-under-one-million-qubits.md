Craig Gidney, May 2025: under one million noisy qubits, in under a week. The 2019
estimate (Gidney and Ekerå) was 20 million. The Global Risk Institute expert survey
puts a cryptographically relevant quantum computer within 10 years at 28% to 49%.

Mosca's rule: if $x + y > z$ (data must stay secret for $x$ years, migration takes $y$
years, a quantum computer arrives in $z$ years), you are already late. Harvest-now,
decrypt-later attacks make $x$ the real driver for banks and governments.
