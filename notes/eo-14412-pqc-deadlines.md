Executive Order 14412 (22 June 2026), as summarised in our research:

- Federal high-value systems use post-quantum key exchange by 2030 and post-quantum
  signatures by 2031.
- Federal contractors comply by the end of 2030.

Why it matters: it turns a vague future risk into a dated sales requirement for every
vendor that sells software to US agencies. That vendor is our candidate customer.

Unverified: the OMB memo said to implement it (M-26-15). Read the order text and add it
to `sources/` before quoting dates on stage.
