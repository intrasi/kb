Article 20 requires makers of L-category vehicles (mopeds, motorcycles, type-approved powered cycles
and quadricycles, except two high-performance subcategories) to fit "designated features to prevent
tampering" that would raise torque, power or top speed, and to "endeavour, through the use of best
engineering practice" to make such changes technically impossible. Delegated Regulation 134/2014 makes
them describe the measures protecting "the computers controlling the environmental and propulsion unit
performance" to the approval authority.

Why it matters: a smaller, faster-moving customer group than car makers with an existing legal duty
on controller software. It is also the easiest demo: a speed limit in a motor controller.

Not verified: ordinary 25 km/h pedelecs fall outside this regulation; check before naming them.

Checked 2026-11-07 on EUR-Lex and legislation.gov.uk.
