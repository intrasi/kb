## Conclusion

All three segments have evidence of protection adoption. The strongest newly inspected CAD evidence explicitly identifies proprietary formulas; SDK evidence includes an on-prem buyer and a dissatisfied anonymous customer who switched suppliers; forensic evidence includes a named obfuscation deployment but also a consolidation correction. None establishes a named company currently paying to solve **AI reconstruction plus obfuscation-maintenance pain** with our proposed system.

## Comparison

| Segment | Observed buying trigger | Plausible first protected asset (our hypothesis) | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAD/engineering calculations | Protect formulas; support framework migration; license/update operations | One proprietary calculation or geometry helper | Numerical semantics and third-party dependencies; much pain is licensing |
| Commercial SDKs | Protect distributed components; on-prem confidentiality; partner requirements; supplier responsiveness | Native document-processing, geospatial or engineering SDK internals | Preserve ABI and host integration; many existing tools integrate well |
| Forensic software | Protect recovery logic; offline deployment and integrity | Bounded parser/recovery helper with synthetic known-output evidence | High correctness expectations; .NET/native mismatch; historical DME is now Magnet |

**My prioritization, not a measured market ranking:** interview CAD/formula vendors for the clearest proprietary-algorithm motivation; seek a small native SDK function for the most bounded technical pilot; keep forensics as a selective follow-up if an accessible vendor reports a present problem.

## Proposed validation

Request a recent release incident, hours spent diagnosing it, current supplier/cost owner, the actual valuable function, and an authorized pilot workload. Measure either better reconstruction resistance at the accepted performance budget or fewer engineering hours at comparable resistance. Separate licensing incidents, intentional RASP responses and transformation errors. Compare against the currently tuned incumbent.

The proof claim must state its exact function/IR scope, assumptions and unsupported cases. It is not an asset-secrecy proof or a certification of the entire shipped application.

## Evidence limits

Targeted primary-source searches and page/PDF inspection on 2026-11-07; selected vendor cases and first-party manuals are not representative surveys. No segment-wide adoption rate, unique supplier census, protection spend, incremental AI theft rate or willingness-to-pay estimate was established. Search results about reconstructing CAD **models from scans** were excluded as a different problem from reconstructing CAD software. Cellebrite's AI commentary surfaced in search but the page returned HTTP 403 and was not used as verified demand evidence. No AI claim was inferred from generic AI features or forensic analyst automation.
