The dollar range is modeled lost US content-production and distribution revenue from global digital-video piracy. It depends on substitution assumptions. It is not app-tampering losses, not streaming-only losses, and not a broad fraud total. The `>80%` figure is the report’s cited streaming share of piracy traffic or consumption, not a loss amount.
