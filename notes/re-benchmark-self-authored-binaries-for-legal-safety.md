**DMCA.** 17 U.S.C. 1201(a): "No person shall circumvent a technological measure that effectively
controls access to a work". The 1201(f) exemption is narrow:
- the copy must be lawfully obtained;
- it covers "solely" interoperability of an independently created program;
- the activity must not otherwise infringe.

**Our reasoning, not a checked fact:** if the team writes and owns every target, or uses OSS it may
redistribute, there is no third-party protection to circumvent, no licence clause against reverse
engineering binds us, and no third-party code is copied.

**Tigress licence:** "free to use for non-profit organizations". For-profit use needs a licence
from the University of Arizona.

Not legal advice. A lawyer should confirm before any commercial release, and for EU law (the
Software Directive), which this note does not cover.
