The checklist gives a 30-second teaser and a 60 to 120 second limit for an investor
meeting that runs much longer. Garaža gives 3 minutes in total, with up to 1 minute of
silent Loom demo ([[garaza-pitch-three-minutes-one-minute-demo]]). Scaled down, the
teaser is the first 20 to 30 seconds and must contain what we do and why it changes
things.

Draft for code protection (edit freely): "AI can now read any app you ship in minutes. We
make your code expensive for AI to read again, and we prove nothing broke."

Start simple, then go deep fast: the checklist's "kindergartners, then graduate students"
pacing.
