- 3 minutes per team, presenting a PPTX deck plus a mandatory product demo.
- The organisers suggest no more than 1 minute of the 3 for the demo.
- The demo is a Loom screen recording **without voiceover**. The narrative stays live.
- 5 minutes of judge Q&A follow.
- "How well you can articulate the product and problem space" is itself judged.

Why it matters to us: the demo must show its point silently inside 60 seconds. For code
protection that means one visual sequence: AI reads the plain function, fails or costs
far more on the protected one, the checker proves both behave the same. Build the UI so
that sequence reads without narration. Plan the 5-minute Q&A answers in advance (market,
incumbents, why a proof matters).
