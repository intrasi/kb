The deal records are Karla's: [[code-protection-vendor-transactions]] (Guardsquare, Jscrambler,
Zimperium) and [[code-protection-evidence-gaps]] (Promon and Digital.ai, amounts not found). This
note reads a pattern across them and adds three records.

**Pattern:**
- Guardsquare: Battery Ventures took a majority stake. "Majority" comes from press coverage
  (tech.eu), not Guardsquare's own release.
- Zimperium: Liberty Strategic Capital took a controlling interest.
- Promon: a GRO Capital-led consortium took a majority stake.
- Digital.ai: TPG formed it by merging CollabNet VersionOne, XebiaLabs and Arxan.

The only disclosed venture round among them is Jscrambler's $15M Series A.

**Added records:**
- Appdome: $13M Series A led by Menlo Ventures, November 2015. Later rounds are unconfirmed.
- RevEng.AI: $15M Series A led by the NATO Innovation Fund with In-Q-Tel, 27 May 2026, from press
  reports. It does defensive binary analysis, not anti-reverse-engineering.
- Verimatrix: listed on Euronext Paris; we have not checked its filings.

Why it matters: exits here have mostly been PE buyouts, and venture rounds are small. Defence and
government money is going into binary analysis. Moderate confidence, because this is our reading
of a small set of deals.
