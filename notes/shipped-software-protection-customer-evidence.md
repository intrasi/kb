## Claim and evidence standard

Public cases show actual reported adoption in specialized software, not merely vendor claims that an industry could benefit. Checked 2026-11-07. These are largely **vendor-published testimonials**, not audited efficacy tests or independently confirmed current contracts. Historical examples establish past category demand; they do not establish present vendor relationships. No source here establishes interest in our proposed startup.

## Offerings and technical evidence, not customer purchases

- [STMicroelectronics partner listing for QShield](https://www.st.com/en/partner-products-and-services/qshield.html): STM32 software/firmware obfuscation, encryption and runtime defenses.
- [Irdeto ActiveCloak for Media, 2023](https://irdeto.com/news/irdeto-launches-renewed-irdeto-activecloak-for-media): DRM SDK combining source obfuscation, white-box cryptography, anti-debugging and integrity checking.
- [BuyDRM SDK security sheet](https://buydrm.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/KeyOS-MultiPlay-SDKs-1.pdf): client SDK obfuscation and runtime/key protections. Product features are not an independent effectiveness result.
- [Irdeto router offering](https://irdeto.com/hubfs/resources/solution-overviews/keys-and-credentials-for-routers.pdf): software protection for router credentials and provisioning.
- [Wibu CODESYS integration](https://cdn.wibu.com/fileadmin/wibu/downloads/booklets/Infoblatt_CoDeSys_CodeMeter_EN-web.pdf): PLC software protection/licensing.
- [ETM WinCC OA release, 2024](https://cdn.wibu.com/fileadmin/wibu_downloads/Press/pressrelease/2024/02-2024/WIBU-PR_ETM_en.pdf): named SCADA protection and licensing case.
- [Historical automotive product brief](https://info.verimatrix.com/hubfs/Datasheets/EN-Verimatrix-Automotive-Market-Brief.pdf): infotainment and digital-key protection use cases. **Vendor ownership update:** [Verimatrix completed its XTD asset sale to Guardsquare on 5 February 2026](https://info.verimatrix.com/hubfs/Finance/Press%20releases%20%28EN%29/2026/Sale%20XTD%20to%20Guardsquare%20Feb5th%202026.pdf). Do not present historical product material as an independent current competitor without checking ownership.
- [MathWorks protected models](https://www.mathworks.com/help/rtw/model-protection.html): third-party distribution of compiled/obfuscated simulation and code-generation artifacts.
- [IEEE 1735-2023](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1735/7237/): protection/rights management for distributed electronic design IP. Hardware designs are adjacent, not ordinary software binaries.
- [Robception installation manual](https://doc.rc-reason.com/v25.10/en/installation_stack.html): CodeMeter runtime integration into a robot-vision deployment with Docker. Deployment licensing evidence, not proof that the container is confidential.

## Implication

There are concrete buyers across multiple industries. The open question is whether our specific workflow beats **their existing protection plus automation and support**. Cases praising straightforward integration are counterevidence to assuming every customer faces high engineering effort. See [[native-sdk-vendors-are-a-plausible-first-obfuscation-market]].
