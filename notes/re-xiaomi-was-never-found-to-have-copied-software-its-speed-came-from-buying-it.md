Searched English-language court reporting and specialist legal press (2026-11-08): no adjudicated
finding anywhere that Xiaomi reverse-engineered or copied software, firmware or an algorithm.
What the record actually holds:
- Design copying is rhetoric, not litigation. Jony Ive called copying "theft and ... lazy" in October
  2014; Apple appears never to have sued Xiaomi over design. Apple's one win, EU case T-893/16, blocked
  EU *registration* of the "MI PAD" trademark.
- The patent cases are royalty and FRAND disputes, not accusations of taking technology. The much-cited
  Ericsson India injunction (December 2014) was vacated in part in April 2016 because Ericsson had
  concealed that Xiaomi's 3G was licensed via Qualcomm.
- The only real software issues are GPL kernel-source non-compliance (2013 onward) and a 2014 incident
  where Xiaomi shipped a renamed fork of Xposed with the attribution removed, then apologised and
  restored credit. Both concern already-public code, not secrets.
- Adverse findings exist on patent scope and trademarks (Gree tower fan, RMB 1.85m, 2024), not copying.

The mechanism behind the speed: minority stakes of 20-40% in partner companies that design and build the
products while Xiaomi supplies brand and channel. Roborock's Xiaomi-related revenue ran 100.0%, then
90.4%, then 50.2% across three pre-IPO years; Ninebot's 55.6%, 73.7%, 57.3%. 210+ invested companies by
the March 2018 prospectus, about 120 of them actually making products. Xiaomi's R&D is roughly a seventh
of Apple's, so it did not outspend incumbents. The SU7 car is the counter-example, done in house at
about RMB 10bn with some 3,400 engineers.

Why it matters: never say "Xiaomi stole it" on stage; it would not survive a hostile question. The true
and stronger line is that Xiaomi proved a competitor no longer needs to steal, because the hardware is
buyable ([[re-incumbent-algorithms-take-years-while-odm-hardware-takes-weeks]]).
Caveats: English-language sources only; Xiaomi's 2025 figures come from search summaries because
ir.mi.com refused direct fetches; do not assert that chip-vendor reference designs explain Xiaomi's
speed, as no source supports it. Checked 2026-11-08.
