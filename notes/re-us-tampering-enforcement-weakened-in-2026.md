Per the research notes (secondary sources, not opened by us): defeat devices were dropped from EPA's top
enforcement priorities in August 2023; diesel tuners were pardoned in late 2025 and July 2026; DOJ stopped
criminal prosecution of diesel tampering on 21 January 2026; a 29 June 2026 memo told EPA to consider
deprioritising civil enforcement against individuals. The Clean Air Act ban itself is unchanged, and
2020-2023 enforcement produced 172 civil cases and $55.5M in penalties.

Why it matters: use the EPA 500,000-truck figure as proof of scale, not as proof of US demand. Euro 7
(from 29 Nov 2026) is the buying reason ([[re-euro-7-makes-ecu-software-anti-tampering-law-from-2027]]).
