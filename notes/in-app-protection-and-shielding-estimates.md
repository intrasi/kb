`EV-CMI-IAP-2026` is CMI’s January 2026 forecast for global in-app protection. It includes shielding and hardening plus RASP, obfuscation, anti-tampering, and services. It is not a verified shielding-only market size and not actual spending. Application shielding is a subsegment. `EV-INSIGHT-SHIELD-2026-NOT-FOUND` is the absent shielding-only 2026 value. The 2025 Insight Partners figure is shielding software, not the CMI total.
