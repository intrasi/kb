## What Not To Build
While the hackathon topic is open format, we do recommend that teams avoid certain industries or topics for building their products, as these spaces are very hard to monetize and also pose very little technical challenges. This does not mean that teams will be disqualified if they build in something related to this, but it will impact the judging criteria.
To showcase what these industries might be, Garaža has built a "What Not to Build" list, which you can use as a reference on what type of projects you should avoid building, as well as a list of industries that you should skip.
Teams should avoid building solutions that are only for regional markets like the Balkan region as these markets misrepresent the actual needs of global tech.
Garaža | What Not to Build
### Industries to avoid
- PetTech - Anything related to pets
- KidTech / FamilyTech / BabyTech - Anything related to kids, families (chores), or baby tech
- EdTech - Anything related to general education, schools, academia. This does not relate to workplace education.
- GovTech - Anything related to government and public services digitization (e.g. digital ID systems, digital health records, tax portals, citizen services, parking systems,  and other municipal infrastructure tools)

## Judging Criteria
# Judging Criteria
Here’s the criteria we use for final evaluation:
1. Team & Execution
How strong is this team and how fast can they execute?
2. Technical Complexity
Did you build something real and technically solid?
3. Is It Something People Want
Is there a clear user need or demand for what you built?
4. Originality
Is the solution new or meaningfully different from existing approaches?
5. Ambition
Could this work as a product on its own, beyond a weekend prototype?
6. How good was the demo and the presentation?
Demo and the presentation show the core value fast. Clear explanation of what, why, and how.

## Pitch Preparation
Teams will showcase what they have built at the Hackathon Demo Day on Sunday, May 24th. You can check the ‣ for the schedule
### Format
Each team will get 3 minutes to pitch, what they’ve built, and what problem they are solving on the Demo Day. 
Teams will present a deck (PPTX) and solution/product demo (mandatory), but we suggest to dedicate not more than 1 minute for product demo.
Pitches are followed by a 5min Q&A from the judges. 
The team should use Loom for recording their solution/product demos, while the rest of the pitch is will be followed with pitch deck on the screen. If you have a high-quality video that you created, you can play it and just record it over with Loom. The whole narrative should be live, screen recording is used for demo only, without voiceover.
