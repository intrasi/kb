## Retrieval and limits

Search families: software vendors + AI reversing + proprietary algorithms; case studies + obfuscation/performance/integration; maintenance + licensing; audio/DSP + AI reversing; industrial practitioners + software-protection surveys. Followed original sources and compared against existing KB notes. Searches are targeted and non-exhaustive, not a population sample. Anonymous social reports, search-engine snippets and general IP-loss estimates were not used to establish named-buyer validation. Relevant primary pages, paper abstracts and selected paper sections were inspected; not every referenced paper was read in full. PDFs above are existing public customer cases viewed with the web PDF reader, not archived in full in this source collection.
