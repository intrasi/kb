## Algorithm licensors

- Bosch Sensortec BSEC2: the BSEC licence covers "all pre-compiled binary blobs"; download only after
  accepting the software licence agreement. https://github.com/boschsensortec/Bosch-BSEC2-Library
- Bosch BSEC integration guide: BSEC is delivered as a pre-compiled static library per platform.
  https://git.digital-infinity.de/DigitalInfinity/ESP32-PaxCounter/raw/commit/17cd82da686440d9e41a3f8a2fab59e9441f43a0/lib/Bosch-BSEC/docs/BST-BME680-Integration-Guide-AN008-47.pdf
- Dirac Research (Sweden) licenses room correction to makers of amplifiers, home theatre, cars and phones;
  2026 hardware deals include Emotiva (1 Sep 2026), McIntosh (22 Jul 2026), Tonewinner (16 Jun 2026);
  Dirac Spaces for car makers launched in the US at AES Detroit 2026. https://www.dirac.com/press

  cost is built into each unit. https://www.minidsp.com/applications/digital-room-correction/dirac-live-vs-rew

  Sunseeker) "winning online"; Husqvarna cited at over 18% global share (Global Market Insights via
  ShelfTrend). https://www.shelftrend.com/home-garden/robotic-lawn-mower-market-analysis-seller-profit-guide-2026
- AnalyticalMR (low reliability) says Husqvarna is late on AI vision and Mammotion and Segway have an
  18-month head start. https://analyticalmr.com/research-insight-detail/leading-companies-and-manufacturers
- Mammotion in 700+ Walmart stores in North America, 4 Feb 2026.
  https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/robotic-lawn-mower-market
- Hearing aids: about 90% of global sales from Sonova, Demant, WS Audiology, GN and Starkey; four launched
  AI flagship platforms within two weeks in August 2026 (e.g. Signia MaX, ReSound Sensia).
  https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/the-race-towards-smarter-hearing-aids
- Chinese hearing aid makers named: NewSound, Vivtone, Austar.
  https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/china-hearing-aids-market-45306
- Guitar modellers: budget makers (Mooer, Hotone, Sonicake) run the open-source Neural Amp Modeler; users
  capture Neural DSP plugins as NAM files. No case of firmware copying found.
  https://www.gearnews.com/neural-amp-modeler-guide-guitar/
